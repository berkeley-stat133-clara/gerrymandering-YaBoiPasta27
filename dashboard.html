Methodology

Data Sources: 2024 votes from CA Statewide Database; 2024 districts from Census TIGER; proposed districts from AB 604.

Method: Area-weighted interpolation allocated precinct votes to new districts proportionally by geometric overlap, assuming uniform voter distribution.

Metrics: Mean-median measures vote packing (±2% neutral); Efficiency gap measures wasted votes (±8% threshold).

Caveats: Single metrics don't prove gerrymandering; geographic clustering affects results; projections assume stable voter preferences.
